Frequently asked questions

Everything you're wondering.

Foundation

What exactly is included in the $1,200 program?

Product sourcing with access to pre-vetted private-label suppliers, packaging design, label design, a Shopify website, full store setup and your brand launch assets — everything listed in the program above.

Do I need to have a brand name already?

No. Many founders come to us with only an idea. We'll help you shape your vision into a cohesive brand identity as part of the process.

Can I choose the products I want to sell?

Yes. You explore our pre-vetted supplier network and choose the products that fit your vision — the brand is yours from day one.

Does the price include manufacturing?

No. Manufacturing, inventory and ongoing marketing are separate. The program covers the brand foundation: sourcing, design, and your online store.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary by project and supplier lead times — we'll map out a realistic schedule together on your first call.

Do you help with marketing after launch?

Ongoing marketing isn't part of the Foundation Program, but we offer creative and growth services you can add when you're ready.

Growth

What is included in the Shopify Revamp + CRO offer?

A storefront redesign, conversion-focused product pages, an improved mobile shopping experience, strategic store improvements and relevant CRO features.

Can you work with my existing Shopify theme?

In most cases, yes. We'll review your current setup and recommend whether to build on your theme or start from a stronger base.

Do you create product photos?

Yes — premium, AI-powered product imagery, studio-style photography and lifestyle creative are available as part of our creative production services.

Can you help improve my conversion rate?

That's the focus of the CRO side of the offer: clearer product presentation, smoother journeys and purchase-focused improvements across your store.

Do you offer ongoing services?

Yes. Beyond the revamp we offer product photography, creative production and marketing support tailored to your goals.

How do we get started?

Reach out through the contact link below — we'll review your store and come back with what we'd improve first.
